 More upgrades of ReflectAI features 

  1. Password reset (table stakes — blocking real users now)
  No "Forgot password?" flow means anyone who forgets their password is gone forever. One email link, one form. Blocks zero revenue and
  loses real signups every day it's missing.

  2. Daily journaling reminders (retention)
  A journaling app lives or dies by habit. Without reminders, users sign up, journal twice, forget. A simple daily email at a user-chosen
  time ("Remind me at 9pm") with a one-click link back to their journal would meaningfully improve D7 and D30 retention. Could use a free
  tier of Resend or Nodemailer with Railway.

  3. Referral system (organic growth)
  "Give a friend one free month, get one yourself" — a simple share link that applies a credit on signup. Zero ad spend, rewards your most
  engaged users, works well for a personal-growth product where people naturally want to share.

  4. Monthly recap email (engagement + perceived value)
  At month-end, send each user a personalised summary: entries written, mood trends, top insight from the month. Makes the product feel
  alive even on days they don't open it.

  5. Journal export (trust + retention)
  Let users download all their entries as a PDF or plain text file. Lowers the barrier to commit to the app long-term — knowing their
  writing isn't locked in builds trust.
