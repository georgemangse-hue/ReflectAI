The Beginning · May 2026
The Spark: "What if journaling could be smarter?"
It started as a single question in a conversation: could AI make journaling genuinely useful — not just a place to dump thoughts, but a tool that reflects back insight, tracks mood, and coaches growth? The target: students and young professionals across Nigeria and beyond.
💬 Initial Idea 🎯 Problem Statement 🇳🇬 Nigerian Market
🏗️
May 24, 2026 · Day 1
Foundation: First Working Version
The initial commit landed with a complete working app — user auth, daily journal entries, mood tracking, AI-generated reflections via Claude, and payment integration with both Paystack and Stripe. Not a prototype: a real product from day one.
🔐 Auth 📝 Journaling 🤖 Claude AI 💳 Payments
📱
May 24, 2026 · Same Day
Mobile-First: PWA Support Launched
Added Progressive Web App support with a service worker, manifest, icons, and smart install banners for both Android and iOS. Users could install ReflectAI to their home screen and use it like a native app — critical for the mobile-first Nigerian audience.
📲 PWA 📴 Offline-Ready 🔔 Install Banner
✨
May 25–26, 2026 · Days 2–3
User Experience: Onboarding, Search, Legal
Rolled out a 3-step first-time onboarding flow, trial access to Pro features so users could experience the value before paying, live keyword search in Entry History, a proper account dropdown, and full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages with Nigerian business details.
🎓 Onboarding 🔍 Search ⚖️ Legal 🆓 Trial
🌍
May 27–29, 2026 · Days 4–6
Market Fit: Geo-Based Pricing for Two Markets
Implemented smart geo-detection to show Nigerian users ₦3,000/month via Paystack or Flutterwave, while international users see $7.99/month via Stripe. Iterated through multiple approaches (server-side, client-side, caching) until the detection was bulletproof. The right price, for the right market, automatically.
₦ NGN 3,000 $ $7.99 🎯 Localisation 🌐 Flutterwave
🧠
May 29, 2026 · Day 6
AI Depth: Conversational Coaching Engine
The reflection prompts evolved into a full AI coaching experience — multi-turn conversations, NLP presuppositions and language patterns woven into the prompts, session completion screens with progress indicators, and exchange limits (6 free / 10 Pro). The journal became a growth partner.
💬 Multi-turn Chat 🧬 NLP Patterns 🏁 Session Endings 📊 Exchange Limits
📈
May 30, 2026 · Day 7
Growth Infrastructure: Referrals & Email
Built a complete referral system: unique codes, credit tracking, a shareable referral page, caps at 3 free months per year, and email notifications when a friend converts. Set up Gmail SMTP for transactional email, and shipped a password reset flow with 24-hour expiry links.
🔗 Referral System 📧 Transactional Email 🔑 Password Reset 🎁 Credits
🔬
June 1, 2026 · Day 9
Research Mode: Listening to the Market
Replaced the generic feedback form with a 9-question market research survey to capture real user insight. Wove African cultural context into every Claude AI system prompt so the coaching feels local and relevant — not like it was built for Silicon Valley. Built feedback analytics for the admin dashboard.
📋 9-Q Survey 🌍 African Context 📊 Analytics 🎙️ User Research
⭐
June 2, 2026 · Now
Today: PDF Export & Ready to Scale
Pro users can now export their entire journal as a beautifully formatted PDF — a meaningful artifact of their growth journey. The product is live, polished, and generating real revenue. What started as a single "what if" conversation is now a complete, paying product used by real people.
📄 PDF Export 💰 Paying Users 🚀 Live Product 🌱 Growing
